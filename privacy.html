Information is used only to operate, secure, and improve the specific App you are using — to sign you in, provide the requested feature, store or sync the content you create, and diagnose issues. I use Google account data solely to authenticate you and to provide the feature you requested, and I do not share it with unrelated third parties. 6. Cookies and local storage

Apps may use cookies or local/browser storage to keep you signed in and remember your preferences. These are used for functionality, not for advertising or cross-site tracking.
