Google Health → Home Assistant

A personal integration by Andrew Nguyen that brings your own health data into your own home lab.

This app connects the Google Health API to a self-hosted Home Assistant instance. It reads your health and fitness data on your behalf and makes it available locally so you can build dashboards, history, and automations from your own data — without your data passing through a third-party cloud service.

What it does

  • Signs you in with your Google Account and reads your Google Health data.
  • Exposes that data as local Home Assistant entities (sensors / history) for visualization and automation.
  • Runs entirely on infrastructure the operator controls.

What data it requests, and why

The app requests read-only access to the specific Google Health data it needs to display — sleep, activity & fitness, nutrition, body measurements, and profile — and nothing more. It uses this data solely to show and track that information for the signed-in user in Home Assistant. It does not sell it, share it with third parties, or use it for advertising.
